Begin with three vertices and a ratio of 0.50 to reveal the classic Sierpiński triangle.

How it works

Choose a random starting point and a random vertex. Move part of the distance toward that vertex, draw a point, and repeat. Restrictions on which vertex may be selected produce different emergent fractals.
